SIA CCTV Operator

The Public Space Surveillance (CCTV) licence qualification. Two units, two multiple-choice exams, plus practical assessments no app can replace.

Format

CCTV unit40 questions, 60 minutes, pass 28 (70%)
Common unit — private security industry72 questions, 110 minutes, pass 70%

What the exam covers

  • Roles and responsibilities of the control room team
  • Legislation
  • CCTV operational procedures
  • Equipment
  • Communications and dealing with incidents
  • Health and safety in the control room
