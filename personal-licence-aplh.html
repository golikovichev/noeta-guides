Personal Licence — APLH

The Level 2 Award for Personal Licence Holders, the mandatory qualification before applying for a personal licence under the Licensing Act 2003.

Format

Questions40
TimeOne hour, as the awarding body specifications set it — some centres quote 40 minutes, so check with yours
To pass28 correct (70%)
WhereThrough an approved centre or an online exam

What the exam covers

  • Licensing objectives
  • The personal licence and key roles
  • Premises, designated premises supervisors
  • Children and age-restricted sales
  • Prohibitions, exemptions and temporary events
  • Offences and enforcement

What a question looks like

One real question from the bank, with the answer and the document it was written from.

According to the NHS, one unit of alcohol is equivalent to how much pure alcohol?

  1. 10ml or 8g of pure alcohol — correct answer
  2. 25ml or 20g of pure alcohol
  3. 50ml or 40g of pure alcohol
  4. 5ml or 4g of pure alcohol

The NHS states that one unit equals 10ml or 8g of pure alcohol, which is around the amount an average adult can process in an hour.

Source: www.nhs.uk/live-well/alcohol-advice/calculating-alcohol-units/

How to book

Book through an approved centre or an online exam provider — there is no central booking service. The qualification is the mandatory step before applying to your council for the licence itself.
