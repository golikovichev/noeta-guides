Life in the UK Test

The test you sit for British citizenship or for settlement (ILR).

Format

Questions24
Time45 minutes
To pass18 correct (75%)
Fee£50
WhereA test centre, on a computer, booked through GOV.UK

What the exam covers

  • The values and principles residents are asked to accept
  • What the UK is: countries, crown dependencies, overseas territories
  • British history from early Britain to the twentieth century
  • Life in Britain today: population, cities, religion, culture
  • Government, the legal system, courts and rights
