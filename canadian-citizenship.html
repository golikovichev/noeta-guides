Canadian Citizenship Test

The written test taken as part of a citizenship application, based on Discover Canada, the only official study guide.

Format

Questions20
Time45 minutes
To pass15 correct
LanguageEnglish or French
AttemptsThree chances to pass
FeeIncluded in the application

What the exam covers

  • Who we are
  • Canada’s history
  • Modern Canada
  • How Canadians govern themselves
  • Federal elections
  • The justice system
  • Canadian symbols
  • Canada’s economy
  • Canada’s regions
