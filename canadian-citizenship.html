What a question looks like

One real question from the bank, with the answer and the document it was written from.

Ordinarily, after an election, who is invited by the Governor General to form the government?

  1. The leader of the party with the fewest seats
  2. The leader of the party with the most seats — correct answer
  3. The Speaker of the House of Commons
  4. The Governor General himself

Ordinarily, after an election, the leader of the political party with the most seats in the House of Commons is invited by the Governor General to form the government.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/federal-elections.html

How to book

There is no booking to do: IRCC invites you to the test after you apply for citizenship. You get three chances to pass, and the test is in English or French.

Five more questions, with answers

Every question below is a real question from the bank, written from the document shown with it. The exam does not publish its own questions, so these are not exam questions — they are what the published text supports.

1. The Prime Minister and the party in power run the government only as long as they have what?

  1. The approval of the Governor General
  2. A majority of seats in the Senate
  3. The support of the provincial premiers
  4. The confidence of the majority of MPs — correct answer

The Prime Minister and the party in power run the government as long as they have the support or confidence of the majority of the MPs.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/federal-elections.html

2. In Canada's federal state, which of these is a responsibility of the federal government?

  1. Education, health and highways
  2. Municipal government
  3. Natural resources and property
  4. Defence — correct answer

In Canada's federal state, defence is one of the responsibilities of the federal government, along with foreign policy, currency, criminal law and citizenship.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/how-canadians-govern-themselves.html

3. According to Discover Canada, what can you do if you have concerns about the conduct of the police?

  1. Nothing, because police conduct in Canada cannot be formally challenged by the public
  2. Bring your concerns to the police, since almost every force has a process for this — correct answer
  3. Only a sitting member of Parliament is allowed to raise a complaint about police conduct
  4. You must hire a private investigator before any concern about police conduct is accepted

The guide states that you can question the police about their service or conduct, and that almost all police forces in Canada have a process for bringing concerns forward and seeking action.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/justice-system.html

4. In what year did Quebec adopt its own flag, based on the Cross and the fleur-de-lys?

  1. 1867
  2. 1931
  3. 1948 — correct answer
  4. 1965

Quebec adopted its own flag in 1948. It is based on the Cross and the fleur-de-lys.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/canadian-symbols.html

5. According to Discover Canada, in what year did the Acadians, the descendants of French colonists, begin settling in what are now the Maritime provinces?

  1. 1755
  2. 1763
  3. 1604 — correct answer
  4. 1867

The Acadians are the descendants of French colonists who began settling in what are now the Maritime provinces in 1604.

Source: www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/who-are-canadians.html
