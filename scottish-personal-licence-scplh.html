What a question looks like

One real question from the bank, with the answer and the document it was written from.

The SQA standard's topic on units of alcohol directs personal licence holders to know about which of the following?

  1. British standard units of alcohol and where to find units-per-drink information — correct answer
  2. The exact blood alcohol limits for driving on Scottish roads
  3. The VAT rates applied to alcoholic drinks at the till
  4. The import duties charged on spirits from overseas

Topic 3.1 covers British standard units of alcohol and strengths of alcoholic drinks, including low-alcohol and no-alcohol distinctions.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

Five more questions, with answers

Every question below is a real question from the bank, written from the document shown with it. The exam does not publish its own questions, so these are not exam questions — they are what the published text supports.

1. What term, used in the SQA standard's topic on alcohol intoxication, describes people drinking heavily before arriving at licensed premises?

  1. Fronting
  2. Pre-loading — correct answer
  3. Stacking
  4. Chain-buying

Topic 3.2 lists 'pre-loading' as an example of a changing drinking habit personal licence holders should be aware of.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

2. Which associated law, separate from the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, governs the role of Environmental Health Officers in licensed premises?

  1. The Food Safety Act 1990 and the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006 — correct answer
  2. The Human Rights Act 1998, as it applies generally
  3. The Data Protection Act 2018, covering personal data
  4. The Sale of Goods Act 1979, covering consumer contracts

The SQA standard references the Food Safety Act 1990 and the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006 as the associated law behind Environmental Health Officers' role.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

3. What is the name of the age-verification challenge scheme named in the SQA standard for personal licence holders?

  1. Think 21
  2. Challenge 25 — correct answer
  3. Check ID 18
  4. ProofPass 21

Topic 2.20 names Challenge 25 specifically as part of applying proof of age.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

4. Which section of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 deals with drunk persons entering or being in premises where alcohol is sold?

  1. Section 97
  2. Section 111 — correct answer
  3. Section 113
  4. Section 115

Section 111 covers drunk persons entering or in premises on which alcohol is sold, the first of a run of related sections.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

5. Besides ensuring compliance and providing guidance, what other duty do Licensing Standards Officers have according to the SQA standard?

  1. Setting the national minimum price for alcohol
  2. Providing a mediation service to avoid or resolve disputes — correct answer
  3. Issuing personal licences directly to applicants
  4. Prosecuting licensing offences in court

Topic 2.6 describes the general functions of Licensing Standards Officers, including providing a mediation service for avoiding or resolving disputes.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf
