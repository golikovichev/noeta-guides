Scottish Personal Licence — SCPLH

The Scottish Certificate for Personal Licence Holders, under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005.

Format

Questions40
TimeNo more than one hour
To pass28 correct (70%)
WhereThrough an approved awarding body

What the exam covers

  • Overview of the licensing function
  • Key roles
  • Licensing and operational responsibilities
  • Protecting children and young persons
  • Control of order
  • Training and associated law
  • Alcohol intoxication and illegal drugs
  • Social responsibility

What a question looks like

One real question from the bank, with the answer and the document it was written from.

The SQA standard's topic on units of alcohol directs personal licence holders to know about which of the following?

  1. British standard units of alcohol and where to find units-per-drink information — correct answer
  2. The exact blood alcohol limits for driving on Scottish roads
  3. The VAT rates applied to alcoholic drinks at the till
  4. The import duties charged on spirits from overseas

Topic 3.1 covers British standard units of alcohol and strengths of alcoholic drinks, including low-alcohol and no-alcohol distinctions.

Source: www.sqa.org.uk/sqa/files_ccc/J50F46.pdf

How to book

Book through an approved awarding body or centre; the exam is arranged by them, not by a central service.
