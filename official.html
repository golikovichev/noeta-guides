Is there an official practice app?

Short answer: for these tests the body that owns the exam publishes the syllabus, the handbook and the booking service — not a practice app. Anything called “the official practice app” deserves a check before you install it.

How to check any app in two minutes

  • Look at the developer name in Google Play, not just the app title. An official app is published by the organisation itself, not by a name that merely repeats the exam.
  • Open the organisation’s own website and see whether it links to the app. If the only place claiming “official” is the store listing, that claim is the app’s own.
  • Read the disclaimer. An honest independent app says it is independent. An app that hides that is telling you something about itself.
  • Check where the answers come from. If questions carry no source you can open, you cannot verify them — and exam facts go out of date (population figures, the monarch, constituency numbers).

Why we are not official, and why that is the point

Noeta is an independent library: seven banks, every question written from a public document, with that document linked under the question. It is not affiliated with any of the bodies above, does not claim to be, and says so in the apps and in every store listing. The value of an independent bank is exactly that it can be checked: if a question’s source no longer says what the question claims, the question is pulled.
